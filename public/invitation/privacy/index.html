Privacy Policy

Last updated August 24, 2026

Phoenix Restored Church Worldwide ("we," "us," or "our") operates this website and the Invitation web app (together, the "Service"). This policy explains what information we collect through the Service, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Contact form. If you submit our Contact form, we collect the name, email address, phone number, topics of interest, and any comments you provide. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry.

Invitation links. Invitation pages may include an identifier in the URL, used to display a personal greeting (name, photo, message, or video) from the member who shared the invitation with you. This identifies the person who sent the invitation — it is not used to identify or track the visitor viewing the page.

Automatically collected information. Like most websites, our server records standard technical information for every request, such as IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, and timestamps. We use these logs only for security, diagnostics, and preventing abuse — not for advertising or building profiles of individual visitors.

Offline storage on your device. The Invitation app uses a service worker and your browser's local cache to save pages and images on your device, so the app loads faster and remains available with a limited or no connection. This storage stays on your device, is used only to improve performance, and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Respond to messages sent through the Contact form
  • Personalize invitation pages with the sender's greeting
  • Keep the Service secure and diagnose technical problems
  • Provide offline access and faster loading of previously visited pages

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or third-party marketing.

How We Share Information

We do not share personal information with third parties except with the service providers below, who process it on our behalf to operate the Service:

  • Mailjet — delivers the email generated when you submit the Contact form. Mailjet processes that submission's contents (name, email, phone, interests, comments) solely to send it to our staff.
  • YouTube — sermon and event videos are shown using YouTube's privacy-enhanced embeds (youtube-nocookie.com), and our sermon listing is built from YouTube's public playlist data. Google's own privacy policy governs any interaction you have directly with an embedded player.
  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces used on this site.

Links to PayPal (for donations), Google Maps (for directions), Instagram, and other external sites take you to services we do not control. We do not receive or process any payment or location data from those sites — their own privacy policies apply once you leave our site.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions exist only as the email generated for our staff; we do not store submissions in a database. Server access logs are retained only as long as needed for security and operational purposes, after which they are deleted or rotated out.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Choices

You can use the Service without submitting the Contact form or following an invitation link. To ask us to delete information you've previously submitted, or with any other privacy question, reach out using the contact details below.

Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect information submitted through the Service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and we'll update the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or how your information is handled? Reach us through our Contact page.
